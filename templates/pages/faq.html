Learn what you can book today and what happens after you request a visit.

What can I book on DocAtDoor?

You can request a home visit with a verified doctor. Nursing, physiotherapy, and diagnostic categories currently open an enquiry form; they are not online booking workflows.

How do I request a doctor's visit?

Create and verify a patient account, search doctor profiles, choose an open time, and enter your visit address and reason. The appointment remains pending until a doctor accepts it.

How long is a visit and what timezone are times shown in?

Visit appointments are one hour. Available starts are offered within the doctor's weekly hours, and the page names DocAtDoor's configured timezone.

Can I change or cancel a visit?

You can cancel pending or accepted appointments from your dashboard. For a visit with a doctor assigned, rescheduling shows currently available times and sends the new request to the doctor.

Who can see my reports and visit notes?

You can access your own records. A doctor can access your report while assigned to an accepted or completed visit; consultation notes are limited to the patient and the doctor assigned to that completed visit.

How do I get a prescription?

After the doctor marks a visit complete, the doctor can add a prescription file. If one is added, you can download it from your dashboard.

What if I have a medical emergency?

DocAtDoor is not an emergency service. Contact your local emergency services if someone may need urgent medical attention.
